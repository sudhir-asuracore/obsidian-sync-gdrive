## 1. Description of the Service

Sync Drive is an open-source client-side plugin that enables users of the Obsidian desktop and mobile application to synchronize their local notes, vaults, and settings with their own Google Drive storage accounts using official Google Drive APIs.

## 3. Encryption Key and Data Responsibility

If you choose to use the optional client-side AES-256-GCM encryption feature:

- Your encryption key/passphrase is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any server.
- **You are solely responsible for backing up and remembering your encryption key.** If you lose or forget your encryption key, your encrypted data cannot be decrypted or recovered by the developer, Obsidian, or Google.
- We recommend maintaining regular offline backups of your notes.
